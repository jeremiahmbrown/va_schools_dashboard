# 2021–22 annual SOL import: QA record

The user supplied the [VDOE SOL School and Division Subject-Area workbooks](https://www.doe.virginia.gov/data-policy-funding/data-reports/statistics-reports/sol-test-pass-rates-other-results)
for 2021–22. The originals are in ignored `data-raw/input/manual/`. The
checked extracts and SHA-256 source hashes are tracked in
`data-raw/annual_sol_2022_school.csv.gz`,
`data-raw/annual_sol_2022_division.csv.gz`, and
`data-raw/annual_sol_2022_sources.json`.

## Source fields and method

Each workbook has one row per school or division, subject, and subgroup. The
import keeps **All Students** rows for English Reading, Mathematics, and
Science. It keeps 5,350 school-subject rows (5,337 reportable) and 402
division-subject rows (399 reportable). The workbooks report pass rates only.
They do not give tested-student numerators or denominators. An explicit `<`
or blank rate stays missing; it is not zero.

The 2022 school subject values are VDOE-reported rates. The 2022 division
subject values are also VDOE-reported rates from the Division Subject-Area
file. The app does **not** average school percentages to estimate a division
rate. School and division `Overall` values are app-defined simple means of
available English Reading, Mathematics, and Science rates. For 2023–26,
division subject values continue to use reported test counts from matched
schools. The change in division calculation method is disclosed in the
[methodology](METHODOLOGY.md); the annual SOL series remains separate from
ACR and School Quality Profiles Percent Passing.

The extractor rejects changed columns, unexpected markers, duplicate source
keys, and rates outside 0–100. The builder matched 1,736 of 1,800 source
schools (96.4%) and 129 of 134 source divisions (96.3%) to the fixed map.
The 2022 Annual SOL Overall value is reportable for 1,736 mapped schools and
129 mapped divisions. Alleghany Highlands has no matching 2022 division row.
Older division rows cannot be combined without test counts, so its 2022
division value remains missing. The local Profile showed this as unavailable.

As a spot check, Accawmacke Elementary has reported English, Math, and Science
rates of 67%, 66%, and 72%, giving an app-defined Overall value of 68.3%.
Accomack County has reported division rates of 67%, 63%, and 61%, giving an
app-defined division Overall value of 63.7%.

`Rscript --vanilla data-raw/extract_annual_sol_2022.R` reproduces the checked
extracts from the original workbooks. The snapshot and year-availability
checks confirm the displayed 2022 values and the separate source method.
