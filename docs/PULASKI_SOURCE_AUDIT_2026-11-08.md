# Pulaski County attendance-boundary source audit - 2026-11-08

[Pulaski County GIS](https://www.pulaskicounty.org/GIS.html) links a school-district locator. Its ArcGIS item `bf25d665a9bc4d658fe88b60682b5a0f` returned "Item does not exist or is inaccessible" for both item metadata and data on 2026-11-08. The County's public [School_Districts Feature Service](https://services5.arcgis.com/cnJiyVVCFyUslPPa/arcgis/rest/services/School_Districts/FeatureServer) has two layers: seven school **points** and five **District** polygons. The polygon fields are `DISTRICT` and `SUPERVISOR`; sample district names are Cloyd, Ingles, Robinson, Massie, and Draper. These are supervisor/election districts, not school attendance areas. Its point layer includes former Pulaski Middle School, so its school points are also not proof of current assignments.

The County's [Community Compass](https://experience.arcgis.com/experience/2cc14a5ab65d4fffb2b5779953f0e587/) describes school-board representatives. Its public configuration references a school web-map item that is inaccessible. A focused search of the school division's site did not find dated attendance polygons. The local research checkout links five elementary schools and one high school to **2015-16 NCES SABS** polygons by checked IDs. The current Pulaski County Middle and virtual school have no matched standard area. These historical links are not current attendance assignments and have not been released from the local research checkout.

Decision: hold a current Pulaski boundary release. Do not use the County supervisor districts as attendance zones. Follow-up Bead `va_schools_dashboard-nlf.7.31` requests adopted elementary and secondary polygons, an effective school year, any single-school or virtual-program assignment rule, and terms for public display and redistribution. The [request draft](PULASKI_VA_ZONE_SOURCE_REQUEST_DRAFT_2026-11-08.md) is saved but has not been sent.
