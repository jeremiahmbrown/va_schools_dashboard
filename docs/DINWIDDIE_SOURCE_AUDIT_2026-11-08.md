# Dinwiddie County attendance-boundary source review - 2026-11-08

[Dinwiddie County GIS](https://www.dinwiddieva.us/330/GIS) links its public data hub. The County's [Operational Layers READ ONLY service](https://services.arcgis.com/pXJ1hE8HkwpsRwne/arcgis/rest/services/Dinwiddie_Operational_Layers_view/FeatureServer) lists `SchoolLocations` points and election districts/precincts, but no school attendance polygon layer. A search of the same public ArcGIS account for school and attendance items did not identify an attendance-zone service. A generic ArcGIS `SchoolDistricts` search result was outside Virginia and is not a Dinwiddie source.

The school division's [attendance-area policy JC-R](https://www.dinwiddie.k12.va.us/app/uploads/sites/2/2019/02/out-of-zone.pdf) says students normally attend the area where they reside. Its [2026 preschool page](https://www.dinwiddie.k12.va.us/preschool-programs-information) refers to a Southside Elementary zone. These confirm that assignment zones matter, but do not provide adopted boundaries, an effective year for each polygon, or public digital reuse terms. A budget-book map shows school locations and a division outline, not five individual elementary areas.

The local research checkout links seven schools to historical **2015-16 NCES SABS** areas by checked school IDs. Those links do not establish current assignments. No current Dinwiddie boundary file was added to production. Bead `va_schools_dashboard-nlf.7.26` remains in progress; the [request draft](DINWIDDIE_VA_ZONE_SOURCE_REQUEST_DRAFT_2026-11-08.md) is saved but has not been sent.
